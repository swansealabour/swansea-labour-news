Welcome |Croeso

“On behalf of the people of Swansea and all my colleagues in Swansea Labour can I welcome you to our wonderful city by the sea. Swansea is a place that is growing and reinventing itself for the 21st Century.”

Cllr Rob Stewart, Leader of Swansea Council

Swansea Labour is the team of Labour councillors and members working for communities right across our city. From our schools, parks and city centre to the cost of living and tackling climate change, we're focused on making Swansea a better place to live, work and enjoy.

Find your local Labour team below, catch up on what's happening in your ward, or get in touch. We'd love to hear from you.
